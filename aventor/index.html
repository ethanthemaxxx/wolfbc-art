Aventor Global is a boutique consulting firm built to be the growth partner of technology companies that want to scale beyond their current markets: Software & IT, Fintech, SaaS, AI and IT nearshoring.

The experience

The site opens on a starfield with a soft cyan glow and a single promise: empowering tech companies to scale globally. Two calls to action split the journey between booking a call and exploring services.

Every service is explained with an animated interface of its own: an initiative tracker for go-to-market rollout, a lead-search feed for commercial acceleration, a campaign scheduler for demand generation and a project card for international expansion. Visitors see the work instead of reading about it.

Growth system

Growth system

The process section turns consulting into a product roadmap: Discovery, GTM Masterplan, Lead Factories MVP and Scale, Close & Follow-Up, each paired with an abstract 3D visual.

The About page extends the same language to the team, keeping one navigation and one booking flow across the whole site.

Design system

Figtree for headlines and body, Fragment Mono for interface details, and a single cyan accent on deep black. Pill badges, glass cards and generous spacing keep a dense B2B offer calm and readable.

Built in Framer, responsive from desktop to mobile, with booking connected to Cal.com.
